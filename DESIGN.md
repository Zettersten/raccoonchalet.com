# Raccoon Chalet visual system

The site feels like a cinematic mountain journal: landscape photography carries the arrival, warm editorial type carries the story, and dark evergreen surfaces create quiet pauses between brighter rooms. Motion follows a single arc from arrival through the retreat rituals to booking.

## Core materials

- Deep evergreen `#101d19` for immersion and nighttime sections.
- Warm paper `#f4f0e8` and sand `#ded8cb` for reading and practical details.
- Ember `#a64f36` for restrained wayfinding and active states.
- Instrument Serif for large, expressive lines; DM Sans for navigation, content, and controls. Both are self-hosted.
- Property photography stays recognizable. Image treatments are gentle and preserve the real setting.

## Composition

The first viewport is a full landscape view with the offer and availability action immediately legible. The page alternates open editorial layouts, a sticky four-chapter photo sequence, a horizontal interior gallery, a portrait film, a room breakdown, and a practical calendar. Booking links are available from the header, hero, calendar, and close.

## Motion

The hero WebGL pass applies a slow current, a pointer-responsive lens, fine grain, and subtle color grading to the deck image. The CSS photo underneath is a complete fallback. The ritual chapters crossfade images as the reader scrolls; short reveal transitions and one marquee provide pacing. Reduced-motion visitors receive visible content, still photography, and no shader loop.

## Interaction and accessibility

Controls are keyboard reachable, use visible focus rings, and keep native labels. The mobile menu contains focus and makes the page inert while open. The calendar offers large date targets, clear status states, and a direct Airbnb path. Availability is presented as a guide because the listing confirms final dates and terms.
