Bryce Resort Public Activities: Booking and Access Guide

Plan Bryce Resort outings from Raccoon Chalet with public booking options, ticket locations, and official sources for access, reservations, and fees.

By Erik Zettersten

Year-round

3 min read

Updated November 8, 2026

Which activities can nonmembers book?

Raccoon Chalet is a vacation home in Basye, Virginia, about five minutes from Bryce Resort. Bryce’s FAQ confirms that nonmembers can purchase public-rate activities. It requests online reservations for skiing and snowboarding, the Bike Park, lessons, tubing, golf and many special events. Before purchasing activities, ask your accommodation provider to confirm whether any resort benefits are included with your booking.

Start with the Hours of Operation page, which separates activity schedules and status information. Find your intended activity, then consult its page and booking options for current public fees and available dates. Public eligibility is not confirmation that an activity will operate during your stay. Avoid arranging the rest of your day around an activity until its availability is confirmed.

What should you reserve for a winter outing?

The Ski & Snowboard page links to slope-use tickets, lift-and-rental packages and ski-school information. Compare those products before paying: identify who needs equipment and who needs instruction, then check exactly what each selection includes. Review the advance-rental booking deadline on that page. For lessons, follow the ski-school link and check the participant’s age and experience against the available options.

Plan tubing separately because the FAQ excludes it from slope-use tickets. The FAQ lists ice skating as an onsite purchase, not an inclusion with skiing. Confirm its operating dates before making it the alternative for nonskiers in your group.

How should riders and golfers arrange their day?

For mountain biking, compare the Bike Park’s day pass, bike-only rental and lift-ticket-and-rental package. Its page also links to lessons. If bringing your own bike, check equipment before loading the car: the park requires suspension and a properly fitted helmet, with full-face helmets required on double-black trails. Review these requirements before deciding whether to bring gear or investigate rentals.

Golfers can use the tee-time reservation link on Get Tickets. Confirm the public rate and booking inclusions before completing the reservation. For driving-range practice instead, that page directs visitors to buy tokens at the Pro Shop.

The Hours page has separate entries for the golf course, shop and practice facility. Check the entry that matches your plan rather than treating a course schedule as confirmation that every golf service is available.

Should you plan Lake Laura as a water outing or a walk?

Treat beach admission, equipment rental and walking as separate planning decisions. Get Tickets identifies the lakeside Beach Shack as the purchase location for day passes and rentals. Confirm beach operations before heading there for either purchase.

Use the Lake Laura page for directions, admission information and rental choices, including kayaks and paddleboards. Its visitor rules state that there is no lifeguard and pets are prohibited. Raccoon Chalet welcomes up to two dogs, so plan pet arrangements before choosing this outing.

For a walk, consult the lake map and trail notes instead of relying on beach information. The page identifies the parking lot at the base of the dam as one trail starting point. Confirm trail access separately from beach operations.

Where do you handle other tickets and booking changes?

For disc golf or scenic lift rides, Get Tickets identifies the Bike Shop as an onsite ticket location. It also provides a scenic-chairlift booking link. Confirm the available purchase method and current charge for your chosen date before joining a queue or completing an online purchase.

Read the FAQ’s cancellation and rebooking terms before paying. If an activity page and booking screen disagree, use the FAQ’s Contact link to resolve the question with Bryce. Save each confirmation and its arrival instructions. For a group splitting between activities, record the meeting point and decide who will handle equipment pickup before leaving the chalet.

Hours, access, conditions, and fees can change. Check the destination’s official information before you go.
