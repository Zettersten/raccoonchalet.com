A relaxed first ski weekend at Bryce Resort

A practical plan for a first ski weekend in Basye: reserve lessons before you arrive, check the mountain on the day, and leave room to unwind afterward.

Winter

3 min read

Updated October 29, 2026

Make the lesson decision before the drive

If anyone in your group is learning, start with Bryce Resort's lesson page rather than leaving the decision for the lift line. The resort currently says ski and snowboard lessons must be booked by 2 p.m. the day before. Its first-time package includes a private lesson, equipment and a lift ticket, with eligibility details on the booking page. Reserve directly with Bryce and confirm the details for your group.

The 2026–27 ski season opening date had not been announced when this guide was checked. Booking a chalet stay and booking a lesson are separate steps, so check the resort's current conditions before making a snow-day plan.

Keep the first morning simple

Raccoon Chalet is about five minutes from Bryce Resort. That short trip makes it easier to give beginners an unhurried start: eat breakfast at the chalet, check the resort's mountain status, then leave time to park, collect any rentals and find the lesson meeting point. The resort asks first-time package guests to arrive at its lift ticket sales area 30 minutes before their lesson to pick up equipment.

For a group with different energy levels, make the mountain the main event and keep the rest of the day open. The resort offers skiing and snowboarding, while the chalet gives anyone ready for a break a comfortable place to return to.

Come back to a warm evening

After the slopes, the chalet's ski gear drop zone keeps the entry routine easy. A cedar sauna, saltwater hot tub and home theater give the group several ways to spend the evening without another drive. Dinner can be a group meal in the kitchen or a low-key night with a movie; the point is to leave room for the mountain day to end at its own pace.
